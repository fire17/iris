Lane verdict. The prototype's mechanism is sound — the registry parses, the live-watch loop fires, the read-only law holds on proof, and the click-to-play routing is intact end to end. What is not sound is what the user actually sees: the registry lanes are off-camera on the home screen, the wall can render entirely black, and the 150 ms open budget is unreachable because the wall defers its select callback behind a fly-to. None of that is data loss or a security hole — it is the product being invisible.
